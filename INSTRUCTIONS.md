# The Ole Rag'n'Cat Pub

## Instructions 

This is a fun mini-game that takes place in a rustic pub. 

Feel free to play with the piano yourself by pressing the keys. 

You may also start or stop the ghost pianist by pressing 'p'. If you interrupt them more that 3 times, they might get angry... 

Spawn ants by pressing 'a'. To get rid of them, try to squish them by clicking on them! 

Finally, a suprise awaits you if you high five the secret cat...
